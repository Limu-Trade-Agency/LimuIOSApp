LIMU Portal and Mobile App Review Meeting Minutes
Date: 14 October 2026
Time: 3:30–4:30 p.m. CAT
Meeting type: Development progress and product review
Participants: Chigomezgo Ndovie — General Manager; Andy — surname and role to be confirmed.
Purpose: Review portal improvements, agree priorities for legacy-portal development, and identify mobile-app changes required before rollout.
1. Review of New Portal Improvements
Andy demonstrated changes arising from the previous review. The client-page field overflow had been corrected, and the KYC form had been reorganized into stages to reduce scrolling. KYC-link generation was tested successfully. Previous employee-module tickets were reported as closed.
The use of an emailed password-reset link instead of an OTP was accepted because it reduced the steps required to reset a password.
Agreements:
Remove the separate background canvas behind the login graphic to improve its alignment.
Retain the staged KYC layout.
Provide a business-category dropdown containing accepted categories.
Use a full-page, staged form for employee creation instead of a slide-out drawer.
2. Consistency of Client and KYC Information
The review identified inconsistencies between the client record, portal KYC form, and mobile-app KYC form. KYC completion had previously updated the status without consistently updating the underlying client information.
Differences included business details, interests, intended imports or exports, and the fields displayed when viewing a client.
Agreement: Align the KYC and client fields across the portal and mobile app. Submitted information should update the corresponding client record, and the client view should display the relevant information consistently. Personal and business accounts should show the appropriate fields.
3. Next Phase of New Portal Development
Andy explained that dedicated pages had been created for areas such as KYC follow-up and client portfolio, which had previously defaulted to client-information views.
Chigomezgo emphasized that these functions needed to be defined with the relevant departments before further development, so that the team focused on useful requirements.
Agreements:
Complete the current corrections before defining the next tickets.
Review operations in the following order: shipments, cargo, checklists, and dispatch and collections.
Continue maintaining the legacy portal during development.
Aim to meet on Monday, 19 October 2026, subject to booking an available slot, to review corrections and agree the next tickets and timeline.

The exact planning-period dates mentioned in this discussion were unclear in the recording and require confirmation.
5. Mobile-App Registration, KYC and Profile Management
The team tested registration, WhatsApp verification, KYC submission, profile editing, and account deactivation or re-registration. The simple registration flow was received positively, and the WhatsApp verification message was received during testing.
Issues identified included an outdated consent link, inconsistent phone-number entries, a business-name update that did not appear correctly, and uncertainty around account reactivation and retained shipment information.
Agreements:
Update the consent link to the current destination.
Correct inconsistencies between mobile-app information and the client record.
Separate ordinary profile editing from changes to submitted KYC information. KYC changes should require a request rather than unrestricted editing.
Review account deactivation and re-registration behavior, including phone verification and the connection to existing client records.
Improve category and interest selection using selectable options rather than editable raw values.
Correct the home-navigation behavior identified during testing.
ID scanning and automatic extraction were discussed as possible future improvements. The team agreed to focus on the existing scope first.
6. Cargo, Packages and Shipment Information
Testing identified problems with the cargo-creation drawer, stage-progress display, package-checking information, shipment assignment visibility, and uploaded package images.
The team also noted that “units verified” and “units checked” were behaving independently and needed review across the portal and app.
Agreements:
Replace the problematic cargo-creation drawer with a more suitable form flow.
Correct progress displays and reconcile checking and verification information.
Show current shipment assignments, package counts, and uploaded images.
Fetch fresh information when users move between pages or tabs so that changes appear promptly.
Remove staff names and internal attribution from customer-facing tracking updates.
Rollout dependency: The app’s shipment experience should not be released before the corresponding shipment-update problems in the portal are fixed.
7. Push Notifications and Notification Preferences
An in-app notification appeared during testing, but a push notification was not received.
Agreements:
Configure iOS and Android apps within the existing LIMU Firebase project and use Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications.
Provide the access needed to complete the configuration.
Allow customers to choose notification channels, including WhatsApp and email, and the types of updates they wish to receive, such as shipment alerts and general updates.
WhatsApp account verification and its budget implications were also raised for follow-up.
8. Navigation and Invoices
The team agreed that invoices needed a more prominent place in the mobile app. Cargo and shipments occupied separate navigation positions despite their close relationship.
Agreements:
Make cargo accessible from within the shipments area.
Use the resulting navigation space for invoices.
Include invoice functionality, including order-form invoices, in the development requirements.
Keep invoice editing on the internal side rather than allowing clients to edit invoices.
The possibility of replacing QuickBooks was discussed. No decision to discontinue QuickBooks was made. Any change would require a separate discussion with Finance and a suitable replacement for the existing invoice workflow.
9. Delivery Plan and Follow-Up
The team agreed that the mobile-app review findings needed to be converted into a concrete, prioritized development plan.
Agreements:
Document the end-to-end tickets and fixes, rank them, and prepare them for planning by Friday, 16 October 2026.
Work toward 31 October 2026 as the target for a tangible, ready set of improvements, with the detailed scope and sequencing established through planning.
Account for dependencies between the app, portal shipment updates, and Client Relations workflows.
A closing discussion about client tiers began, but the recording ended before a complete agreement was captured.
